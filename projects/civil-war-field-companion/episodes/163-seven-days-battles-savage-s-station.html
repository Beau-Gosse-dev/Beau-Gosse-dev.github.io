SEVEN DAYS' BATTLES: SAVAGE'S STATION

The Battle of Savage’s Station was tactically indecisive. John B. Magruder attacked the United States rear guard late on June 29 but committed only part of his command and did not stop the withdrawal. Edwin V. Sumner’s troops held until darkness, then crossed White Oak Swamp. The Confederates occupied the field and captured the sick and wounded left at the station, but Robert E. Lee failed to trap the retreating army north of the swamp

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Brig. Gen. Richard Griffith

Commander of a Mississippi brigade in Magruder’s force. Mortally wounded by artillery fire during the late-afternoon attack at Savage’s Station and died shortly afterward in Richmond. The National Park Service identifies him as mortally wounded in the battle; Library of Congress cataloging for his surviving letter gives June 30 as the date of death, while some later accounts give June 29.
