What happened

Before the attack

Hood ordered an attack on Thomas

Hood directed Hardee's and Stewart's corps to attack from right to left. The target was Thomas's Army of the Cumberland after it crossed Peachtree Creek but before its line was fully established.

About 3:30 p.m.

Hardee's attack began late and shifted right

William Bate's division moved through rough ground into the gap between Thomas and Schofield and missed the main U.S. line. The shift separated Confederate divisions and prevented a simultaneous attack.

Afternoon

Walker and Maney attacked Newton

W. H. T. Walker's and George Maney's divisions attacked John Newton's IV Corps division. U.S. infantry and artillery stopped repeated attacks. Newton bent back part of his line when Confederate troops overlapped his right flank.

Afternoon

Stewart attacked the XX Corps line

William Loring's division struck John Geary's sector, briefly overrunning the forward 33rd New Jersey. Edward Walthall's division penetrated parts of Geary's and Alpheus Williams's positions before U.S. counterattacks and enfilading fire forced it back.

By dark

The Confederate attack ended

Stewart withdrew his troops to their starting lines. Thomas's army held the ground, and Hood did not prevent the U.S. armies from continuing their movement against Atlanta.
