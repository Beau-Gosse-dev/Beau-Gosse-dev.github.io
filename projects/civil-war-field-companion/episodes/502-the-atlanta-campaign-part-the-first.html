People and command

United States

President and commander-in-chiefAbraham LincolnSet national war policy and sought a general able to coordinate all theaters
Military Division of the MississippiUlysses S. GrantDirected western operations and developed the coordinated strategy; promoted to general-in-chief in March 1864
Army of the TennesseeWilliam T. ShermanLed the Meridian expedition and later received command of Grant’s western military division
Army of the CumberlandGeorge H. ThomasHeld Chattanooga and prepared the largest army for the advance into Georgia
Army of the OhioJohn G. FosterTried to move against Longstreet in eastern Tennessee
Army of the OhioJohn M. SchofieldReplaced Foster and prepared to join Sherman’s spring campaign
U.S. Military RailroadsDaniel C. McCallumRepaired, expanded, and operated the rail network supplying the western armies

Confederacy

Confederate president and commander-in-chiefJefferson DavisDirected Confederate military policy
Army of TennesseeJoseph E. JohnstonDefended north Georgia from his base at Dalton
Detached First Corps, Army of Northern VirginiaJames LongstreetRemained in eastern Tennessee with about 20,000 men
