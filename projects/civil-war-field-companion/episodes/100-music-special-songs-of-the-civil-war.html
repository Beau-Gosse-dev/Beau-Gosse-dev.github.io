MUSIC SPECIAL: Songs of the Civil War

A guide to more than twenty songs heard during the Civil War. The episode explains how patriotic, sentimental, religious, minstrel, emancipation, marching, and camp songs shaped wartime politics and morale. It also identifies the composers and the events that changed how several songs were understood
