Episode 114 · April 6, 1862, about 5–9 a.m. · Fraley Field, the Corinth Road, Peabody’s ridge, Spain Field, and Prentiss’s camps at Shiloh

SHILOH (Part the Fourth)

Peabody’s unauthorized patrol delayed the Confederate advance long enough to alert the forward camps. Hardee’s first line began moving at about 6:30 a.m. Wood and Shaver overran Peabody’s brigade, while Gladden and Chalmers broke Miller’s brigade at Spain Field. Prentiss’s division was routed before 9 a.m., but the resistance delayed and disorganized the attackers. Johnston incorrectly concluded that he had already turned Grant’s left and shifted reserves westward

Important leaders killed or wounded

Commander, 1st Brigade, Prentiss’s division. Killed by a shot to the head at about 8:30 a.m. after suffering four earlier wounds

Confederacy

Brigadier General Adley H. Gladden

Brigade commander on the Confederate right. Mortally wounded in the arm and shoulder by artillery fire at Spain Field; died April 12
