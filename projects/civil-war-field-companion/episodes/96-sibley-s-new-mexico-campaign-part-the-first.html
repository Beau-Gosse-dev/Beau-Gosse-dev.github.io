Episode 96 · February–August 1861 · Texas and southern New Mexico Territory, from San Antonio to Fort Bliss, Mesilla, and Fort Fillmore

SIBLEY'S NEW MEXICO CAMPAIGN (Part the First)

Texas authorities took control of U.S. Army posts and property after David Twiggs surrendered the Department of Texas. John R. Baylor then marched west with the 2nd Texas Mounted Rifles, occupied Mesilla, and forced Major Isaac Lynde to evacuate Fort Fillmore. Baylor captured more than 500 U.S. soldiers near San Augustine Springs on July 27, 1861, and proclaimed a Confederate Territory of Arizona south of the 34th parallel on August 1
