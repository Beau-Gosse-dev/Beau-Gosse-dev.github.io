Episode 102 · February 22–March 6, 1862 · Fayetteville, Elm Springs, Bentonville, Little Sugar Creek, and Pea Ridge in northwest Arkansas

PEA RIDGE (Part the Second)

Van Dorn failed to trap Franz Sigel at Bentonville. Curtis concentrated the Army of the Southwest behind prepared defenses at Little Sugar Creek, but Van Dorn rejected a direct attack and ordered an overnight march around the United States right flank by the Bentonville Detour
Confederate march beganMarch 4, 1862
Planned marchAbout 38 miles from the Boston Mountain camps through Fayetteville to Bentonville
Load ordered per soldierMusket, 40 cartridges, one blanket, and three days’ rations
Sigel’s rear guardAbout 600 men after the 2nd Missouri left Bentonville
Vandever’s march42 miles in 16 hours to reach Little Sugar Creek
Bentonville DetourAbout eight miles around the United States right to Cross Timber Hollow
