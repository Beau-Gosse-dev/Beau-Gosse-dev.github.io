Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Jabez L. Drake

Colonel of the 33rd Mississippi Infantry. Killed while leading the regiment in Featherston’s brigade. He and men from the regiment were buried on the battlefield.

Confederacy

Bailey M. Talbot

Captain in the 57th Alabama Infantry. Killed during the attack. Joel Murphree’s letter describes the initial uncertainty over whether Talbot had been killed, wounded, or captured.
