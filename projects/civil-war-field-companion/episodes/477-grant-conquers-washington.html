Episode 477 · March 8–26, 1864 · Washington, D.C.; Brandy Station and Culpeper, Virginia; Nashville, Tennessee

GRANT CONQUERS WASHINGTON

Grant arrived in Washington on March 8, received his lieutenant general’s commission on March 9, and became general-in-chief on March 10. Halleck moved to the new position of army chief of staff, and Sherman replaced Grant as commander of the Military Division of the Mississippi. After meeting Meade, Grant kept him in command of the Army of the Potomac. Grant also abandoned his plan to direct the war from the West, established field headquarters near Culpeper, and maintained direct access to Lincoln and Stanton while preparing coordinated offensives
