Episode 178 · August 30, 1862 · The Second Manassas battlefield around Groveton, the unfinished railroad, Brawner Farm, Dogan Ridge, Chinn Ridge, and the Warrenton Turnpike in Prince William County, Virginia

SECOND MANASSAS (Part the Third)

Confederate victory. Porter’s and Hatch’s afternoon assault briefly pressed Jackson’s line at the unfinished railroad but was repulsed. Longstreet then struck the exposed Federal left, destroyed Warren’s small brigade, and began the counterattack that drove Pope’s army from the field; this episode ends before the fighting on Chinn Ridge and Henry Hill is completed

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Col. William S. H. Baylor

Commander, Stonewall Brigade. Killed while leading the depleted Stonewall Brigade into the threatened Deep Cut sector. The National Park Service account states that he died with the 33rd Virginia’s flag wrapped around his body.

United States

Col. Henry S. Roberts

Commander, 1st Michigan Infantry. Killed during Porter’s assault. The 1st Michigan belonged to Charles W. Roberts’s brigade in the center of the Federal formation advancing toward the unfinished railroad.
