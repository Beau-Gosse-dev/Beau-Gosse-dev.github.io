Episode 157 · June 1–26, 1862 · Richmond and the Chickahominy River approaches in Virginia, with Jackson moving from the Shenandoah Valley through the Virginia Central Railroad corridor toward Ashland and Mechanicsville

LEE TAKES COMMAND

Robert E. Lee replaced the wounded Joseph E. Johnston on June 1 and prepared an offensive against George B. McClellan. Lee strengthened the Richmond defenses, used J. E. B. Stuart’s reconnaissance to confirm that the Federal right north of the Chickahominy was exposed, transferred Stonewall Jackson’s command from the Valley, and issued a plan for June 26. The offensive had not begun by the episode’s endpoint. Its success depended on Jackson’s timely arrival, a relay through Lawrence O. Branch, multiple river crossings, and coordination among commanders who had not operated together as one army

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Gen. Joseph E. Johnston

Commander of the army defending Richmond. Seriously wounded at Seven Pines on May 31 by a rifle bullet and shell fragment. He survived, but the wounds removed him from command and directly led to Lee’s appointment on June 1.
