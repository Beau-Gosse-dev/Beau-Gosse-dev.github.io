CLASH OF THE IRONCLADS (Part the Fourth)

Monitor and Virginia fought for about four hours on March 9, but neither penetrated the other’s armor or disabled the opposing ship. The immediate engagement was a tactical draw. Monitor protected the grounded Minnesota and the United States blockade remained in place, giving the United States the strategic advantage. Virginia withdrew around 12:30 p.m.; Monitor did not pursue because its orders centered on Minnesota

Step 1 of 38

Virginia prepared to return

Before dawn on March 9, its crew inspected damage from the previous day. The smokestack, fittings, two gun muzzles, and the bow were damaged, but 98 hits on the armor had not penetrated.
