Episode 115 · April 6, 1862, about 5–10 a.m. · Shiloh Church, Ray Field, the farm lane through the United States center, Bell Field, and the Hamburg–Savannah Road, Tennessee

SHILOH (Part the Fifth)

Prentiss re-formed part of his routed division, and the divisions of W. H. L. Wallace and Stephen Hurlbut extended the new United States center. Johnston diverted Chalmers and Jackson toward Stuart’s isolated brigade after mistaking it for a full division. On the United States right, Sherman recovered from the initial surprise and held near Shiloh Church for more than two hours. By about 10 a.m., seven Confederate brigades had converged on Sherman and Julius Raith, and Hildebrand’s brigade began to break
