Episode 164 · June 30, 1862 · White Oak Swamp, the Glendale crossroads, and the roads extending south to Malvern Hill and the James River, east and southeast of Richmond, Virginia

SEVEN DAYS' BATTLES: GLENDALE (Part the First)

This episode covers the deployment and opening operations of June 30 rather than the main infantry fighting at Glendale. By the point where the episode ends, William B. Franklin had blocked Thomas J. “Stonewall” Jackson at White Oak Swamp, Benjamin Huger had been delayed on the Charles City Road, and four United States brigades had left the White Oak sector to reinforce the line near Glendale. The larger Battle of Glendale, completed in episode 165, was indecisive; the United States line of retreat remained open

People and command

United States

Army of the PotomacMaj. Gen. George B. McClellanInspected the Glendale area, then moved to Haxall’s Landing without appointing an overall battlefield commander
VI Corps at White Oak SwampMaj. Gen. William B. FranklinDivisions of Brig. Gens. William F. Smith and Henry W. Slocum covered the swamp exits
II CorpsMaj. Gen. Edwin V. SumnerDivisions of Brig. Gens. Israel B. Richardson and John Sedgwick occupied separated sectors from White Oak Swamp to Glendale
III CorpsMaj. Gen. Samuel P. HeintzelmanDivisions of Brig. Gens. Philip Kearny and Joseph Hooker held ground on McCall’s right and left, respectively
Pennsylvania Reserves division, V CorpsBrig. Gen. George A. McCallHeld the central western approach to the Glendale crossroads while most of Porter’s corps was near Malvern Hill
IV CorpsBrig. Gen. Erasmus D. KeyesReached the Malvern Hill area ahead of the formations still guarding Glendale and White Oak Swamp

Confederacy

Army of Northern VirginiaGen. Robert E. LeeDirected separated columns toward the Federal rear and the Glendale crossroads
Column pursuing through White Oak SwampMaj. Gen. Thomas J. “Stonewall” JacksonIncluded divisions under D. H. Hill, Richard S. Ewell, and W. H. C. Whiting; stopped north of the swamp after an artillery engagement
Division on the Charles City RoadMaj. Gen. Benjamin HugerWilliam Mahone’s leading brigade encountered extensive obstructions and cut a new route through the woods
Main striking force on the Long Bridge RoadMaj. Gens. James Longstreet and A. P. HillApproached the Federal line west of Glendale; their assault is covered in episode 165
Reserve moving by the Darbytown RoadMaj. Gen. John B. MagruderDirected to support Longstreet and A. P. Hill if needed
Division moving by the River RoadMaj. Gen. Theophilus H. HolmesAssigned to threaten the wagon route near Malvern Hill and restrict Federal reinforcement from the south
