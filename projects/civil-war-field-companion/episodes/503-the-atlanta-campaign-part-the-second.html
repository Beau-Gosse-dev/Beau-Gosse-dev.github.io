Secretary of WarJames A. SeddonCollected army condition reports and acted on supply problems
Army of TennesseeJoseph E. JohnstonAssumed command December 28, 1863, and rebuilt the army at Dalton
Senior corps commanderWilliam J. HardeeCommanded temporarily after Bragg and designed the furlough system
