Episode 106 · August 1861–March 9, 1862 · Washington, D.C.; Brooklyn, New York; the Atlantic coast; and Hampton Roads, Virginia

CLASH OF THE IRONCLADS (Part the Second)

The United States Navy approved John Ericsson’s ironclad design, built USS Monitor in about 100 days, and commissioned the ship under John L. Worden. Monitor survived severe flooding and toxic fumes during its trip from New York and reached Hampton Roads on the night of March 8. It took position beside the grounded USS Minnesota before CSS Virginia returned the next morning
