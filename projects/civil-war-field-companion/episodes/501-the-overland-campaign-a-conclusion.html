V CorpsGouverneur K. WarrenPerformed capably but lost Grant’s confidence through hesitation and disputed orders

Important leaders killed or wounded

United States

John Sedgwick

VI Corps commander. Killed by Confederate fire at Spotsylvania Court House on May 9, 1864

Confederacy

J. E. B. Stuart

Army of Northern Virginia cavalry commander. Mortally wounded at Yellow Tavern on May 11 and died in Richmond on May 12, 1864

Confederacy

James Longstreet

First Corps commander. Seriously wounded by Confederate fire in the Wilderness on May 6, 1864; survived but left the army for months

United States

James S. Wadsworth

V Corps division commander. Mortally wounded in the Wilderness on May 6 and died May 8, 1864
