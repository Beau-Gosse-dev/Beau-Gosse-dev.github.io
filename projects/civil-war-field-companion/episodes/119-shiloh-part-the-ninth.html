Episode 119 · April 6, 1862, about noon through nightfall · Jones Field and the western Shiloh battlefield; Crump’s Landing, Stoney Lonesome, the Shunpike, River Road, and Pittsburg Landing, Tennessee

SHILOH (Part the Ninth)

Sherman and McClernand counterattacked from Jones Field around noon or 12:30 p.m. Fourteen regiments advanced, surprised the Confederate left, and drove part of it back nearly half a mile before ammunition shortages forced a return to Jones Field. The attack delayed and further disorganized Hardee’s forces. North of the battlefield, Lew Wallace received Grant’s written order at about 11:30 a.m., allowed a meal break, and marched on the Shunpike toward Sherman’s former position. After learning after 2 p.m. that the United States line had moved, he countermarched, crossed to the River Road, and reached Grant’s army after dark following a march of at least sixteen miles
