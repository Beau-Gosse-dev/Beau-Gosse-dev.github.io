Episode 481 · May 5, 1864 · Saunders Field, Orange Turnpike, Orange Plank Road, and the Brock Road intersection, Virginia

THE WILDERNESS (Part the Third)

Grant and Meade initially believed they faced only part of Lee’s army, partly because U.S. cavalry had not located the Confederate columns. Warren attacked Ewell at Saunders Field before his flanks were covered; the attack broke down in the woods and was repulsed. Three miles south, Getty reached the Brock Road intersection just before Heth and stopped the Confederate advance. Getty then attacked alone shortly before 4 p.m. while Hancock’s II Corps was still arriving
