SPOTSYLVANIA (Part the First)

Grant moved southeast instead of retreating after the Wilderness, but Lee anticipated the move. Anderson’s Confederate infantry marched through the night, reinforced Stuart’s cavalry at Laurel Hill, and stopped Warren’s V Corps short of the Spotsylvania crossroads. Grant then allowed Sheridan to leave with most of the U.S. cavalry to fight Stuart
