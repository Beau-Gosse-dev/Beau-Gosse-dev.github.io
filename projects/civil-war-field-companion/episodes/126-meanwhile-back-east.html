"MEANWHILE, BACK EAST..."

George B. McClellan took command of the disorganized United States forces around Washington after First Bull Run and turned them into the Army of the Potomac. He restored discipline, expanded the army, organized brigades and divisions, developed a large staff, and strengthened the capital’s fortifications. The work made Washington more secure and won McClellan intense loyalty from many soldiers, but his inflated estimates of Confederate strength and conflict with General-in-Chief Winfield Scott encouraged delay. By October, McClellan had proposed an army of 273,000 for a broad offensive and resisted pressure for an immediate advance. The episode’s opening looks ahead to the failed Peninsula Campaign and the August 3, 1862 order to withdraw his army from Harrison’s Landing
