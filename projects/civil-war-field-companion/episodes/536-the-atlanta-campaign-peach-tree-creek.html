What happened

The armies were positioned north and east of Atlanta

Stewart held the Confederate left, Hardee the center, and Cheatham the right. Thomas was crossing Peachtree Creek while Schofield and McPherson moved toward Decatur.

Hood identified a gap in the U.S. line

About two miles separated the left of Thomas from the right of Schofield. Marshy stream headwaters made rapid support between the two forces difficult.

Hood met his senior commanders

On the night of July 19, Hood met with Stewart, Hardee, Cheatham, and Georgia militia commander Gustavus W. Smith.

Hood ordered an attack on Thomas

Hardee and Stewart were to attack Thomas after his corps crossed the creek but before they had completed their defenses.

The plan called for an attack from east to west

Hardee was to begin on the Confederate right. The divisions to his left would follow in sequence and push Thomas west toward the angle between Peachtree Creek and the Chattahoochee River.

The attack was set for 1 p.m.

Cheatham, Wheeler, and the Georgia militia were assigned to protect the eastern approaches while Hardee and Stewart attacked.

U.S. troops had already reached Decatur

Hood did not know how far McPherson and Schofield had advanced east of Atlanta.

Hood shifted the Confederate line to the right

After Wheeler requested help, Hood ordered Cheatham to move toward the Decatur Road. Hardee and Stewart also had to move right before attacking.

The shift delayed the attack

Cheatham moved farther than Hood expected. Hardee and Stewart followed, and the movement was not complete until after 3 p.m.

Peachtree Creek complicated the U.S. crossing

The stream ran west through steep banks and dense vegetation about four miles north of Atlanta. Thomas crossed at several points.

Thomas established a three-mile front

Palmer’s XIV Corps held the right, Hooker’s XX Corps the center, and John Newton’s IV Corps division the left. Most of the army was south of the creek by noon.

The first Confederate attacks began

Rice C. Bull of the 123rd New York described the sudden firing. The battle opened at about 3:15 p.m.
