THE BURNSIDE EXPEDITION (Part the Second)

United States Army and Navy forces destroyed the remaining Confederate gunboats near Elizabeth City, captured New Bern after breaking the Confederate line at the railroad brickyard, and forced Fort Macon to surrender. New Bern remained a United States base for the rest of the war, but Burnside left with 7,000 men in July before attacking the railroad junction at Goldsboro
Battle of New BernMarch 14, 1862; United States victory after about four hours
Burnside’s force at New BernAbout 11,000 soldiers supported by 13 warships
Branch’s force at New BernAbout 4,500 North Carolina troops
Confederate defensive lineAbout one mile from Fort Thompson to the railroad, with a gap near the brickyard
New Bern losses stated in episodeU.S.: 90 killed, 380 wounded; Confederate: 64 killed, 101 wounded, 413 missing
Fort MaconBombarded April 25; its garrison capitulated April 26
Coastal resultNew Bern, Morehead City, Beaufort, and Fort Macon entered United States control
Unfinished objectiveThe expedition did not advance 60 miles inland to the Goldsboro railroad junction
