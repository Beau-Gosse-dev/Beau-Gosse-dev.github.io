Episode 101 · December 25, 1861–early March 1862 · Rolla, Lebanon, and Springfield, Missouri, to Fayetteville and the Boston Mountains in northwest Arkansas

PEA RIDGE (Part the First)

Samuel Curtis drove Sterling Price and the Missouri State Guard out of Missouri. Curtis then kept the Army of the Southwest in northwest Arkansas, where Earl Van Dorn prepared to attack it with the combined forces of Price and Ben McCulloch
United States force at LebanonJust over 12,000 men and 50 artillery pieces
Price at SpringfieldAbout 7,000 men
Route of advanceThe Telegraph Road from Rolla through Springfield into northwest Arkansas
Distance Price retreatedAbout 100 miles from Springfield into the Boston Mountains
Curtis’s supply lineNearly 200 miles back to the railhead at Rolla
Confederate advantage after concentratingAbout 7,000 more men than Curtis, according to the episode
