SHILOH (Part the Sixth)

Sherman abandoned the line near Shiloh Church at about 10 a.m. and joined McClernand along the Hamburg–Purdy Road. Grant reached Pittsburg Landing at about 9 a.m., organized ammunition and straggler control, ordered Lew Wallace and William Nelson toward the battlefield, and met Sherman around 10 a.m. A renewed Confederate attack broke Marsh’s and Raith’s brigades, then forced Sherman, McClernand, and Veatch to retreat toward Jones Field. The attack lost momentum while intermingled Confederate units reorganized and soldiers searched the captured camps
