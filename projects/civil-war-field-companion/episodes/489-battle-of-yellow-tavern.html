BATTLE OF YELLOW TAVERN

Sheridan defeated Stuart’s smaller force at Yellow Tavern, mortally wounded the Confederate cavalry commander, fought through the Meadow Bridges, and reached the James River after damaging two railroads and Confederate supplies
