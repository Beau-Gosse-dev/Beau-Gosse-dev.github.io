SHILOH (Part the First)

The loss of Forts Henry and Donelson forced Confederate troops out of Kentucky, Nashville, and Columbus. Albert Sidney Johnston and P.G.T. Beauregard concentrated about 40,000 men at Corinth. Grant’s army moved up the Tennessee River while Buell marched from Nashville. Johnston decided to attack Grant near Pittsburg Landing before the two United States armies could unite
