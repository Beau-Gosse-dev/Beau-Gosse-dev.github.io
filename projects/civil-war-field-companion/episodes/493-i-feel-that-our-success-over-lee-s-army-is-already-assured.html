Episode 493 · May 24–28, 1864 · North Anna River to Hanovertown and Haw’s Shop, Virginia

"I FEEL THAT OUR SUCCESS OVER LEE'S ARMY IS ALREADY ASSURED"

Grant withdrew from Lee’s North Anna position, crossed the Pamunkey at Hanovertown, and continued southeast; the seven-hour cavalry fight at Haw’s Shop gave both armies reliable information about the other’s position
