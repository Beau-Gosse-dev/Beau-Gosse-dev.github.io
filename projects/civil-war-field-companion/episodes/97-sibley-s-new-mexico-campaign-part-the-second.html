Episode 97 · February 21, 1862 · Valverde Ford on the Rio Grande, about six miles north of Fort Craig, New Mexico Territory

SIBLEY'S NEW MEXICO CAMPAIGN (Part the Second)

Confederate troops under Colonel Thomas Green captured six U.S. artillery pieces and forced Colonel Edward Canby’s field force back to Fort Craig. The Confederates held the battlefield but did not capture the fort, its garrison, or its supplies. Henry Sibley continued north with Fort Craig and more than 3,000 U.S. troops behind him
Sibley’s invasion force2,515 men and 15 artillery pieces at the start of the advance
Fort Craig garrisonAbout 3,800 regulars, New Mexico volunteers and militia, and Colorado volunteers
BattleFebruary 21, 1862
Final Confederate assaultAbout 750 dismounted Texans
U.S. guns capturedSix
Casualties stated in the episodeU.S.: 111 killed, 160 wounded, 204 missing; Confederate: 71 killed, 157 woundedPublished totals vary by source. The National Park Service gives 263 U.S. and 187 Confederate casualties.
Immediate resultConfederate victory; Fort Craig remained in U.S. hands
