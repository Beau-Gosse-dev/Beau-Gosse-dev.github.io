Episode 97 · February 21, 1862 · Valverde Ford on the Rio Grande, about six miles north of Fort Craig, New Mexico Territory

SIBLEY'S NEW MEXICO CAMPAIGN (Part the Second)

Confederate troops under Colonel Thomas Green captured six U.S. artillery pieces and forced Colonel Edward Canby’s field force back to Fort Craig. The Confederates held the battlefield but did not capture the fort, its garrison, or its supplies. Henry Sibley continued north with Fort Craig and more than 3,000 U.S. troops behind him

Step 1 of 23

Canby took command in New Mexico

Edward R. S. Canby replaced William W. Loring after Loring resigned from the U.S. Army. Canby reorganized the department while officers and regular troops were leaving for service elsewhere.
