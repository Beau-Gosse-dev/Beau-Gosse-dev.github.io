Episode 486 · May 11–18, 1864 · Mule Shoe salient and Bloody Angle, Spotsylvania Court House, Virginia

SPOTSYLVANIA (Part the Third)

Hancock’s assault captured the northern half of the Mule Shoe, 20 guns, and about 3,000 prisoners. Confederate counterattacks stopped the advance while Lee’s army built a new line across the base of the salient and withdrew to it early on May 13. Later U.S. attacks did not break that new line
