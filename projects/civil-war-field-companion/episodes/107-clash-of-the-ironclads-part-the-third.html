Episode 107 · March 6–8, 1862 · Gosport Navy Yard, the Elizabeth River, Newport News Point, and Hampton Roads, Virginia

CLASH OF THE IRONCLADS (Part the Third)

CSS Virginia surprised the United States blockading squadron on March 8. It rammed and sank USS Cumberland, forced USS Congress to surrender and then burned it, and fired at the grounded USS Minnesota before withdrawing because of darkness and the falling tide. More than 250 United States sailors were killed. Virginia lost two men killed, and its commander Franklin Buchanan was wounded. USS Monitor arrived that night
