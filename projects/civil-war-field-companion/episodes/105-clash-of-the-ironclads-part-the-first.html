Episode 105 · April 1861–March 8, 1862 · Gosport Navy Yard, Norfolk, and Hampton Roads, Virginia

CLASH OF THE IRONCLADS (Part the First)

The Confederacy rebuilt the burned steam frigate USS Merrimack as the ironclad CSS Virginia. On March 8, 1862, Franklin Buchanan took Virginia into Hampton Roads and attacked the United States blockading squadron, sinking USS Cumberland and destroying USS Congress. USS Monitor arrived that night; the duel between the ironclads is covered in episode 106

The naval battle coincided with Pea Ridge
