Episode 488 · May 2–19, 1864 · Southwestern Virginia and New Market in the Shenandoah Valley

THE COLLAPSE OF GRANT'S PERIPHERAL STRATEGY (Part the Second)

Breckinridge defeated Sigel at New Market and sent about 2,500 troops to reinforce Lee. Crook and Averell had already withdrawn to West Virginia, so Grant’s western Virginia operations no longer kept Confederate forces away from the Army of Northern Virginia
