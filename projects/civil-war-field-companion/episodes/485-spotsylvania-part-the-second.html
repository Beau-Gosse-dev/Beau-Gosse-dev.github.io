Episode 485 · May 8–11, 1864 · Laurel Hill, Po River, and Mule Shoe salient, Spotsylvania Court House, Virginia

SPOTSYLVANIA (Part the Second)

Lee retained the Spotsylvania crossroads and repulsed four separate U.S. attacks on May 10. Upton’s 5,000-man column briefly broke the Mule Shoe defenses, captured prisoners and guns, and withdrew when the supporting attacks failed. Grant used that temporary breakthrough as the model for a larger assault
