Episode 173 · August 18–24, 1862 · The Rapidan and Rappahannock river corridors from Culpeper and Gordonsville to Freeman’s Ford, White Sulphur Springs, Rappahannock Station, Warrenton, and Catlett Station in central and northern Virginia

STALEMATE ALONG THE RAPPAHANNOCK

Indecisive along the Rappahannock. Pope escaped Lee’s planned turning movement at the Rapidan and held the north and east bank of the Rappahannock. Confederate probes did not establish a permanent crossing, but Stuart’s raid captured intelligence that confirmed Pope’s dispositions and the approaching Federal reinforcements, helping Lee plan the next phase of the Northern Virginia Campaign

Important leaders killed or wounded

United States

Brig. Gen. Henry Bohlen

Brigade commander, 3rd Division, I Corps, Army of Virginia. Killed by a shot through the heart on August 22, 1862, while his three-regiment force retreated from Trimble’s and Hood’s Confederates at Freeman’s Ford. His men recrossed under fire, and some drowned in the river.
