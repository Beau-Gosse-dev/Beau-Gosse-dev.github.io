SHILOH (Part the Fourteenth)

United States victory. Grant and Buell attacked at dawn with reinforced armies, recovered the ground lost on April 6, and forced Beauregard’s outnumbered army back toward Shiloh Church. Breckinridge covered the Confederate withdrawal down the Corinth Road until late afternoon. The United States armies held the battlefield, while the main Confederate force returned to Corinth and Grant mounted only a limited pursuit

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Albert Sidney Johnston

Commanding general, Army of the Mississippi. Mortally wounded in the right leg on April 6 and bled to death on the field; Beauregard commanded the army on April 7.

United States

W. H. L. Wallace

Commander, 2nd Division, Army of the Tennessee. Mortally wounded while attempting to escape the Hornets’ Nest encirclement on April 6; died at Savannah on April 10.
