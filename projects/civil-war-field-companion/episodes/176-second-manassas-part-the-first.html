Episode 176 · August 28, 1862 · Thoroughfare Gap in the Bull Run Mountains and the Brawner Farm–Groveton area along the Warrenton Turnpike, northern Virginia

SECOND MANASSAS (Part the First)

Two different results shaped the day. Longstreet forced the lightly held passage at Thoroughfare Gap and continued toward Jackson. At Brawner Farm, Jackson’s evening attack on Rufus King’s division ended in a tactical stalemate, but it fixed Pope’s attention on Jackson and led Pope to concentrate for battle on August 29

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Lt. Col. Lawson Botts

Commander, 2nd Virginia Infantry, Stonewall Brigade. Killed while commanding his regiment in the Brawner Farm fighting on August 28. He was one of the two Stonewall Brigade regimental commanders whose deaths the episode mentions without naming.

Confederacy

Col. John F. Neff

Commander, 33rd Virginia Infantry, Stonewall Brigade. Killed near the Brawner Farm line on August 28. He was found after the firing close to the point where his regiment had advanced.
