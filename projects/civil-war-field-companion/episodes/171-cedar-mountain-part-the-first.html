CEDAR MOUNTAIN (Part the First)

Operational movement without a battle in this episode. Robert E. Lee sent Thomas J. “Stonewall” Jackson to protect Gordonsville, reinforced him with A. P. Hill’s Light Division, and authorized a limited offensive. On August 7 Jackson began moving toward Nathaniel Banks’s isolated II Corps near Culpeper

Step 1 of 34

The campaign transition is summarized

The episode connects the end of the Peninsula Campaign with the formation of Pope’s army and the movement toward Second Manassas.

Army of VirginiaMaj. Gen. John PopeI Corps under Franz Sigel, II Corps under Nathaniel P. Banks, and III Corps under Irvin McDowell; ordered the August concentration at Culpeper
II Corps, Army of VirginiaMaj. Gen. Nathaniel P. BanksThe forward Federal formation near Culpeper; approximately 11,000 men in the episode’s account
Cavalry brigade attached to Banks’s corpsBrig. Gen. John P. Hatch, then Brig. Gen. John BufordHatch failed to conduct the intended rapid railroad raid; Pope replaced him with Buford before the August operations
Army of the PotomacMaj. Gen. George B. McClellanHeld Harrison’s Landing until ordered to move the army from the Peninsula toward a junction with Pope
Federal force transferred from the North Carolina coastMaj. Gen. Ambrose E. BurnsideAbout 14,000 men in the episode’s account moving north by water to support operations in northern Virginia
