Episode 103 · March 6–7, 1862 · Bentonville Detour, Elkhorn Tavern, Leetown, Oberson’s Field, and Morgan’s Woods at Pea Ridge, Arkansas

PEA RIDGE (Part the Third)

On the first day at Pea Ridge, the battle split into separate fights at Elkhorn Tavern and Leetown. The Leetown attack failed after Ben McCulloch and James McIntosh were killed and Louis Hébert’s isolated brigade was repulsed. Most of McCulloch’s division left the field without reaching Van Dorn and Price at Elkhorn Tavern

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Benjamin McCulloch

Division commander at Leetown. Killed instantly by United States skirmishers while reconnoitering ahead of the 16th Arkansas.

Confederacy

James McIntosh

Cavalry commander and McCulloch’s successor. Killed while advancing with the 2nd Arkansas Mounted Rifles minutes after McCulloch’s death.

United States

Eugene A. Carr

Commander of the 4th Division at Elkhorn Tavern. Wounded three times during the March 7 fighting; remained in command.
