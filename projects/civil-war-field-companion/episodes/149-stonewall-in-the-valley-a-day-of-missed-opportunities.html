Episode 149 · May 23–25, 1862 · Northern Shenandoah Valley, Virginia: Strasburg, Cedarville, Middletown, Newtown (present-day Stephens City), Kernstown, and Winchester

STONEWALL IN THE VALLEY: A DAY OF MISSED OPPORTUNITIES

Confederate forces pierced Nathaniel Banks’s retreating column at Middletown and captured men, horses, wagons, and stores, but Thomas J. “Stonewall” Jackson failed to destroy Banks’s main force. Delayed intelligence, divided columns, a two-hour turn south against a small rear guard, looting, darkness, and exhausted troops allowed most of Banks’s command to reach Winchester. Jackson and Richard Ewell closed on the town overnight, setting up the First Battle of Winchester on May 25

The episode opens on May 24
