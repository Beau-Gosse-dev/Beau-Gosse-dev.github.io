Episode 172 · August 7–11, 1862; battle fought August 9 · Cedar Mountain battlefield along the Culpeper–Orange Road south of Culpeper Court House, Culpeper County, Virginia

CEDAR MOUNTAIN (Part the Second)

Confederate victory. Banks’s outnumbered II Corps struck first and broke part of Jackson’s left, but arriving brigades from A. P. Hill’s Light Division restored the line and drove the Federals toward Culpeper. Jackson stopped the pursuit after dark and withdrew across the Rapidan on August 11 as the rest of Pope’s army concentrated

Important leaders killed or wounded

Confederacy

Brig. Gen. Charles S. Winder

Commander of Winder’s division. Mortally wounded by a Federal shell while directing artillery near the road; died a few hours later on August 9, 1862

Confederacy

Capt. Joseph Carpenter

Commander of Carpenter’s Battery. Struck in the head by a shell fragment near Winder’s position; lingered for several weeks before dying of the wound

United States

Brig. Gen. Christopher C. Augur

Commander of the 2nd Division, II Corps. Wounded during the attack south of the Culpeper–Orange Road; Brig. Gen. George S. Greene assumed division command
