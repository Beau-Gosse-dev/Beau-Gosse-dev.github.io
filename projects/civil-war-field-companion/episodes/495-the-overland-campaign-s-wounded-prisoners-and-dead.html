THE OVERLAND CAMPAIGN'S WOUNDED, PRISONERS, & DEAD

The campaign produced tens of thousands of casualties, overwhelmed the first U.S. evacuation route, sent more than 19,000 captured soldiers to prison, and left at least 12,000 dead for wartime and postwar burial details to recover
