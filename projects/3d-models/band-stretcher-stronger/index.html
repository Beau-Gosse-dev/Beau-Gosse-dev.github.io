A stronger drum for the same inserts

Revision 3 supports 23 mm of the insert spine in stepped sockets, with 26 mm rear walls and 4 mm side walls. The low front wall leaves the largest band opening clear. The diagonal frame and thicker rims carry over from Revision 2. Existing inserts still fit: only the drum needs a new print.

Choose your files

Print drum.stl upright as supplied. Use slot_inserts_16-20mm.stl for all five pairs, including 19 mm. Split the file into objects in your slicer to print just the pair you need. The editable OpenSCAD file opens as an assembly; export the separate parts for printing.

Printing & use

Start with 0.2 mm layers, four perimeters, and solid inserts. Inspect the upper-rim bridges and brace junctions in your slicer. Match the slot to the band body and wider end fittings, smooth every contact edge, and check bath clearance and filament compatibility. The 254 mm reference arc is not a guaranteed stretched band length.

Before you print
Prototype. Revision 3 passed the supplied mesh and CAD clearance checks but has not been physically tested. Strength, fit, and cleaning performance still need confirmation.
