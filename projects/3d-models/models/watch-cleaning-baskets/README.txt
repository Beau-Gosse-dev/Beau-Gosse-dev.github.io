This is a remix of muddtt's watch cleaning baskets with some minor refinements.  https://www.printables.com/model/301422-rotor-for-pearl-watch-cleaning-machinehttps://www.printables.com/model/301419-pearl-supreme-watch-cleaning-basketThe rotor top needs a 3 mm threaded insert and screw.The layer holding the mini baskets was designed for 24x22mm baskets.I recommend using ABS filament, as it has chemical and heat resistance.To get the meshed bottoms on the applicable baskets, see the Cura settings below.EDIT 1: Added an extension spindle to get the basket further into the jars as well as the jar holder brackets and drying station parts.
